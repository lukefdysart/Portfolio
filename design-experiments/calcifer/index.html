Calcifer — Logo Lab

Three logo directions for Calcifer — a servo-driven physical heart that beats in sync with a live human pulse. Each mark is shown at hero scale and at project-icon scale, with the CALCIFER wordmark. All artwork is editable inline SVG. A comparison strip at the bottom places the concepts beside the real portfolio logos (MIMIC · MARY · Stories Illuminated).

Comparison with the existing portfolio logos

The three live portfolio marks (copied read-only from index.html) share a 128 × 128 badge format: a 104 × 104 rounded-square card (rx 30) on a saturated gradient, light foreground artwork, and a small DYS monogram. The Calcifer concepts below are placed in a neutral dark badge of the same scale so you can judge dimensions, visual weight, and styling side-by-side. Calcifer currently runs lighter/line-based vs. the filled gradient badges — useful to decide whether to adopt the badge treatment.
