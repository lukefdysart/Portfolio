Calcifer — Logo Lab v5 · final direction

One focused mark: a realistic anatomical heart glowing like an ember in a dark hearth. On each heartbeat the heart and its fireplace glow flare bright against the dark card — strong contrast, like a fire catching — then settle to embers. Calcifer is present as two ember-eyes glowing deep inside, with a slow blink and a few rising sparks. Carries the corner DYS tag and the portfolio's rounded badge so it still ties to MIMIC / MARY / Stories.
