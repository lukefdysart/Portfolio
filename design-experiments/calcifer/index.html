Calcifer — Logo Lab v2

Three new, deliberately distinct directions for Calcifer — a working physical model of a human heart, driven by servos linked to the maker's real heart rate. The brief: a Studio-Ghibli "eccentric inventor's workshop" warmth, with anatomy as the foundation, believable brass servo mechanics, an integrated DYS maker's mark, and a gentle looping heartbeat. All artwork is editable, layered inline SVG on warm backgrounds.

Animation loops indefinitely and auto-disables under prefers-reduced-motion.

Scale & DYS reference — the existing portfolio logos

Copied read-only from the live index.html (not redesigned). They establish the portfolio's scale and, importantly, the DYS maker's-mark convention — a small, quiet monogram set into the corner. The Calcifer concepts carry DYS forward as an engraved brass plate / wax seal / banner. Note the existing logos use dark gradient badges; Calcifer intentionally moves to warm, light, badge-free marks per the new brief.
