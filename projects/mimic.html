Project MIMIC

A robotic hand that moves with you.

MIMIC translates human hand movements into real-time robotic motion. Using computer vision to track finger positions, the system estimates individual finger bends and sends those values to an Arduino-controlled robotic hand — a direct connection between human movement and physical machinery, without gloves, controllers, or wearable sensors.

Overview

A camera watches your hand. Computer vision locates the joints of each finger, and from those positions MIMIC estimates how far each finger is bent. Those bend values drive a physical robotic hand so that it follows along with your own hand in real time.

Technical approach

  • Hand landmarks are detected from a live camera feed with computer-vision hand tracking.
  • For each finger, the angles between its joints are measured and mapped to a bend percentage from fully open to fully closed.
  • The per-finger bend values are sent to an Arduino-controlled robotic hand, which actuates to match.
  • No gloves, controllers, or wearable sensors are required — the camera is the only input.
