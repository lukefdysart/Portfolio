About

LUKE DYSART

I'm Luke Dysart, a junior studying Electrical & Computer Engineering and Biomedical Engineering at Vanderbilt University, originally from Chantilly, Virginia. I've never been particularly drawn to following a conventional path. Whether it's engineering, research, or art, I'm usually more curious about what hasn't been done than what already has.

At Vanderbilt, I contribute to combat casualty surgery research in the Biomedical Modeling Lab. Before transferring, I studied at CU Boulder, where my team won the Electrical, Computing, and Energy Engineering Design Expo.

I was part of the Colorado Buffaloes' 2025 National Championship team and now serve as Secretary of Vanderbilt Club Hockey. As the son of two Marines, service has always been part of my life, and I'm working toward becoming an Air Force Combat Rescue Officer.

A surprising amount of what I've done started on a whim, just to see where an idea might lead. I couldn't tell you what I'll take on next, and I wouldn't have it any other way. I'm also still known to make a good playlist.

Robotics · Computer Vision · Human–Machine Interaction

Project MIMIC

A robotic hand that moves with you.

MIMIC translates human hand movements into real-time robotic motion. Using computer vision to track finger positions, the system estimates individual finger bends and sends those values to an Arduino-controlled robotic hand.

The result is a direct connection between human movement and physical machinery, without gloves, controllers, or wearable sensors.

Interactive Storytelling · Projection · Animatronics

Stories Illuminated In Development

What if the illustrations could tell the story, too?

Stories Illuminated explores how projection, animatronics, and physical storytelling can transform a traditional storybook into an interactive experience.

With the projection system concealed inside the book, illustrations can appear to move and come to life. Inspired by Dolly Parton's Imagination Library, the project brings engineering and storytelling together in a new approach to reading.

To the People Who Made Me

I am who I am because of the people who came before me and the people who stood beside me.

I've been fortunate enough to have a support system that I couldn't have asked to be better. Family, friends, teammates, mentors, teachers, and people whose paths crossed mine for only a moment have all shaped who I am. Some of you may never realize how much you changed my life, but I carry those experiences with me every day.

The early mornings, late nights, hard workouts, and seemingly impossible goals become a lot easier when I remember the people who believed in me, challenged me, and showed me what was possible. I know that the opportunities I've had and the things I've accomplished are built on the time, sacrifices, and encouragement of so many others.

I want to give everything I am to the people and communities that made me.

I want to chase the ideas that seem absurd, attempt the things people say can't be done, and hopefully make the impossible feel a little more possible for someone else. Not simply to prove that I can, but because so many people have done that for me.

Whether you've known me my entire life or were only part of it for a day, thank you. Every conversation, opportunity, and act of kindness has contributed to the person behind this portfolio.

I hope that somewhere along the way, I can give someone else even a fraction of the confidence you've given me.

And to the people I haven't met yet, I hope I can do the same for you.
